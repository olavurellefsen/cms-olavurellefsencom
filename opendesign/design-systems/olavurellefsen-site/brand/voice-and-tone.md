# Voice and tone

- Use plain, calm English and sentence case.
- Name the content object directly: “Current work”, “Work URL”, “Add work item”.
- Make state explicit: “Added to draft”, “Removed from draft”, “Publish to make this public”.
- Prefer verbs over CMS jargon: Add, move up, move down, remove, publish, discard.
- Avoid exclamation marks, emojis, novelty labels, and technical schema/path language in the interface.
- Destructive-looking actions must explain reversibility when they only affect a draft.
